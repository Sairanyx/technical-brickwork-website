What we collect, and why

Almost everything we hold comes from you filling in the quote form. There is no account to create and nothing you have to tell us beyond what we need to price the job and get back to you.

What Why we need it Lawful basis
Name So we know who we are speaking to Legitimate interests
Phone number To call or WhatsApp you back about the job Legitimate interests
Email address (optional) To send a written quote if you prefer email Legitimate interests
Postcode To check the job is in an area we cover and to price travel Legitimate interests

Where we rely on legitimate interests, the interest is straightforward: you have asked us for a price, and we cannot give you one without being able to contact you and understand the job. You can object to this at any time. See your rights below.

Please don't send us sensitive information

The quote form is for describing brickwork. Please do not include health information, financial details, card numbers, or anything about other people who have not agreed to it. We do not ask for any of that and we do not need it to quote for a job.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs of requests made to the site: the page requested, the time, the response, browser type and IP address. This is ordinary web server activity, used to keep the site online and secure and to diagnose faults. We do not use it to build a profile of you or to identify individual visitors.

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. Our Cookie Policy sets out the detail, including the third-party services the site loads.

In practice that means the International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, as set out in each provider's terms.

We may also share information where we genuinely have to: with our accountant for company accounts and VAT, with our insurers if a claim arises, or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Records Kept for
Enquiries that do not lead to work 24 months from your last contact with us, then deleted
Customer and job records 6 years after the end of the financial year the work falls in
Invoices and VAT records 6 years, as HMRC requires
Server logs Held for a short period by our hosting provider under their retention schedule

We keep customer and job records for six years because brickwork can be the subject of a claim years after it is finished, and because tax law requires it. If you ask us to delete an enquiry before the 24 months are up, we will, unless we need it for one of the reasons above.

Keeping it secure

The site is served over HTTPS, so anything you submit is encrypted in transit. The enquiry database sits behind access controls, and the admin page used to view enquiries is protected by a login. Access is limited to the people in the business who need it, which is a very short list.

No website can promise perfect security, but if a breach occurs that is likely to put your rights at risk, we will tell you and report it to the Information Commissioner's Office as the law requires.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct it if something is wrong or out of date.
  • Delete it, where we have no continuing reason to keep it.
  • Restrict how we use it while a question about it is being sorted out.
  • Object to our using it where we rely on legitimate interests.
  • Receive it in a portable format, where that right applies.

Email info@technicalbrickwork.co.uk and we will respond within one month. There is no charge. We may ask you to confirm who you are before we hand over personal information.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK regulator, at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Marketing

We do not send marketing emails or texts, and we do not add enquiries to a mailing list. If you hear from us, it is because you contacted us about a job.

Children

This website is aimed at people arranging building work, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information about anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, for example by starting to use a new service provider, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
