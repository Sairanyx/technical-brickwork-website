Who We Are

Your Vision Is Our Vision

Technical Brickwork · a family-run bricklaying business based in Harrow, London. Built on trust, craftsmanship, and a genuine care for every job we take on.

Founded in 2017, we have spent over eight years delivering quality brickwork across London and beyond. From small repairs to full extensions, every project receives the same level of precision and attention to detail.


Our core team handles the majority of work directly. For larger projects, we bring in trusted, vetted workers to ensure the same quality standard regardless of scale.

What We Do

Our Services

Anything to do with bricks, we do it. From a single wall repair to being part of a large commercial project as a specialist subcontractor.

New Builds and Extensions

Bricklaying and Construction

Constructing walls, extensions, new builds, chimneys, and custom feature walls. Stonework and blockwork included.

  • House extensions and new builds
  • Chimneys and fireplaces
  • Feature walls and stonework
  • Blockwork structures

Maintenance and Restoration

Repair and Repointing

Repointing, brick replacement, crack repairs, and structural restoration. We match mortar colour and preserve the original look.

  • Repointing and crack repair
  • Facade and wall renovation
  • Brick cleaning and waterproofing
  • Graffiti removal
  • Structural wall repairs

Outdoor and Landscaping

Garden Features

Transforming outdoor spaces with quality brickwork. Garden walls, patios, BBQ areas, fire pits, steps, and retaining walls.

  • Garden walls and boundary walls
  • Patios and paving surrounds
  • Outdoor kitchens and BBQ areas
  • Fire pits and seating areas
  • Retaining walls and steps

Commercial Work

Subcontracting and Commercial Projects

We work as specialist brickwork subcontractors on larger commercial developments alongside principal contractors, developers, and construction firms.

  • Specialist subcontracting on large sites
  • Residential developments
  • Retail and commercial units
  • Public and infrastructure projects
  • Long-term contractor partnerships
